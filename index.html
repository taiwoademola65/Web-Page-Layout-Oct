Google Analytics

Google Analytics is the go-to platform for millions of website and app owners seeking to gain a deeper understanding of their website and app performance. With Google Analytics, you can fine-tune your digital strategy, optimize your campaigns, and take your online presence to new heights.

Looking for your Google Analytics account or reports? Sign in to Google Analytics to view your properties and dashboards, or visit the Google Analytics Help Center for product support. This site provides technical documentation for developers and learners.

Add Google Analytics to a website (gtag.js)

Install the Google tag (gtag.js) to measure website interactions, compare tagging options, set up mobile app SDKs, and log custom events.

Query reports with the Data API (runReport)

Build custom dashboards and automate reporting with the Analytics Data API, manage accounts and properties with the Admin API, or analyze raw event data with BigQuery Export.

Send server-to-server events (Measurement Protocol)

Complement client-side tagging by sending events directly from your servers to Google Analytics and validating event payloads before deployment.
